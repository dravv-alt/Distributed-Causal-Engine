Reading the records correctly

Execution tells you whether the procedure finished. Result tells you what it measured. Review tells you what a human approved. These are separate.

Earlier pending/draft files remain historical. The dated E003 approval, E004 registration and 8 October E004/E002-R002 review amendments explain later authority; they do not approve every roadmap stage.

REVISE means the evidence calls for reconsideration. UNKNOWN means the evidence does not support a meaning. Coverage means the component was available to rank; it is not diagnostic accuracy.
