The idea in your words

When a website slows down, ask: “Which part started the problem?”

A request may go through an API to a database. A cache or rate limiter may be the right fix—but only after we have evidence that the database load or abusive traffic was really the cause. DCE’s future job is to show that evidence. It must not guess and automatically make infrastructure changes.

User requests→API server→Database Possible protections: cache · rate limit · alert · human review
