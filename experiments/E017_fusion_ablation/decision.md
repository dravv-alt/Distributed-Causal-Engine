# E017 decision

**Disposition:** synthetic ablation recorded; application-level fusion effectiveness remains UNKNOWN.

In these generated cases, full fusion scored 89.8% Top-1 versus 63.2% for the strongest independent modality (symbolic-only), a paired bootstrap difference of +26.7 percentage points (95% percentile interval +23.0 to +30.3). The neural+topology+temporal arm scored 90.3%, 0.5 points higher; an exploratory paired interval for full fusion minus that arm spans -3.3 to +2.3 points. The pilot does not show a benefit from adding the synthetic symbolic-like score to the three-modality arm. Retain the ablation result as a warning that adding a modality can dilute useful evidence under equal weighting.

Do not promote the generated score to RCAEval or DCE effectiveness. The next stage XP015 can proceed as a separate synthetic component-ablation pilot; real-system claims remain unestablished.
