# E021 decision

**Disposition:** intervention evidence distinguished the two declared synthetic mechanisms; empirical causal claims remain unsupported.

The observational correlation rule had 52.5% balanced accuracy and falsely declared a causal link in 55.3% of common-cause cases. The intervention rule had 100% balanced accuracy and zero false causal calls in this sample. The synthetic potential outcomes were constructed to make the intervention contrast identifiable; this is a mechanism sanity check, not evidence of a DCE causal system.

Proceed to XP019 under a separate controlled-intervention pilot, while retaining the limits of simulated interventions.
